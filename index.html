    How does it work?

    TestPal is a retrieval chatbot. It does not call any online AI service.

    1. Your message is split into words and stemmed (testing → test).
    2. Each word gets a TF-IDF weight: rare, meaningful words count more than common ones.
    3. Your message is compared with every question in its knowledge base using cosine similarity.
    4. If the best match is similar enough, it answers and shows its confidence. If not, it says it is not sure and suggests related topics.
